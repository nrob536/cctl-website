Membership

Choose the option that suits your whānau. Memberships are for borrowing toys in person at the library.

Household Membership

$85 for 12 months
Up to 5 toys at a time

For families wanting regular access throughout the year, with no additional per-toy hire charges.

Grandparents / Kaumātua Membership

$60 for 12 months
Up to 3 toys at a time

A lower-cost option for grandparents and kaumātua who want to borrow toys for time spent with their mokopuna.

Quarterly Membership

$30 for 3 months
Up to 3 toys at a time

A shorter-term option for families who prefer a lower upfront cost or want to try the service before committing to a full year.

Returning Members

To recognise the reduced service available during 2025, returning 2025 members will receive a 15% discount if they rejoin by 1 February 2027.

Payment Plans

Payment plan options are available for Household and Grandparents / Kaumātua memberships. Families can begin borrowing after paying an initial $30, with the remaining balance ($55) due within six weeks to complete the annual membership payment.

Volunteer Discounts

Discounted volunteer membership fees will be considered on a case-by-case basis by the Committee.
